================================================================================
HEROES OF LUMORA - 2D Game Sprites
================================================================================

UNIVERSAL SPECIFICATIONS
========================
Art Style: Pixel art, 2D, modern flat top-down view (not isometric)
Base Size: 16x16 pixels per sprite (can be scaled up by AI)
Background: Solid color background (not transparent). Colored background will be removed
in post-processing.
Spacing: Each sprite centered with clear spacing around it for easy selection
Detail Level: Clean, readable at small scale, no tiny labels or text
Perspective: Flat overhead view looking straight down
No watermarks, or images other than the sprites

STYLE CONSISTENCY:
- All sprites should have consistent shading/lighting direction
- Use similar color depth and saturation across all sprites
- Maintain consistent line weight (pixel thickness)
- Keep anti-aliasing minimal for pixel art feel

TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS:
- Generate at 2x or 3x the final size (so 64x64 or 96x96) then scale down
- This maintains crispness and detail at small sizes
- Ensure transparency is clear (no white backgrounds)
- High contrast so sprites are readable at 32x32

COLOR RECOMMENDATIONS:
- Use vibrant but natural colors
- Avoid pure white (use off-white)
- Avoid pure black for outlines (use dark gray)
- Keep saturation high enough to be readable small

ORGANIZATION:
- Create each prompt separately for best results
- Request horizontal sprite strips (easier to work with)
- Ask AI to space sprites evenly
- Get high-res versions you can scale down

PROMPT 14: Portals Spritesheet - Monster Spawn Rifts
================================================================================

2D pixel art spritesheet top-down view. Create 6 different portal/rift sprites
where monsters spawn from. Transparent background. Each sprite 32x32-48x48,
centered with spacing.

PORTAL STYLE: Each portal should look like a magical/interdimensional tear or
vortex. Top-down perspective showing the opening/swirl. Animated appearance
with swirling energy. Portal shows it's a dangerous spawn point.

---

PORTAL 1: VOID PORTAL
Dark cosmic portal. Black and deep purple swirling vortex. Has tentacle-like
void energy reaching outward around edges. Otherworldly and menacing. Spawns
undead/cosmic monsters (Skeleton, Ghost, Wraith, Lich). Dark void energy
spiraling inward.

PORTAL 2: INFERNAL PORTAL
Fiery hellish portal. Red, orange, yellow flames swirling in vortex pattern.
Hot, dangerous appearance. Demonic energy radiating outward. Spawns fire/demon
monsters (Balrog, Wizard, evil fire creatures). Crackling energy effect.

PORTAL 3: TOXIC PORTAL
Poisonous corrupted portal. Green and sickly yellow-brown swirling. Dripping
or oozing effect. Noxious gas/poison visible. Bio-hazardous look. Spawns
poison/corruption monsters (Hezrou, corrupted creatures). Bubbling energy.

PORTAL 4: ELDRITCH PORTAL
Mysterious alien portal. Purple and cyan/pink swirling mix. Strange geometric
patterns around edges. Alien, unnatural appearance. Reality-bending look.
Spawns beholder/eye creatures and otherworldly entities. Crystalline energy.

PORTAL 5: CORRUPTED NATURE PORTAL
Twisted nature portal. Sickly green and black swirling. Corrupted plants/vines
wrapping around portal edge. Organic but wrong-looking. Nature corrupted.
Spawns corrupted nature creatures and twisted beasts. Thorny/vine effects.

PORTAL 6: SHADOW PORTAL
Pure darkness portal. Black and dark gray swirling void. Shadowy mist/fog
effect. Ominous and subtle compared to others. Shadow energy seeping outward.
Spawns shadow creatures, incorporeal entities, and dark beings. Misty tendrils.

---

ANIMATION SUGGESTION:
Each portal should have a swirling/rotating effect. Consider creating 3-4
frame animation strip for each portal showing the rotation/pulse effect.
Monsters will appear at center of portal when spawning.

COLOR PALETTE SUMMARY:
- Portal 1 (Void): Black, Deep Purple, Dark Blue
- Portal 2 (Infernal): Red, Orange, Yellow, Black
- Portal 3 (Toxic): Green, Yellow-Brown, Sickly Yellow
- Portal 4 (Eldritch): Purple, Cyan, Pink, White
- Portal 5 (Corrupted Nature): Green, Black, Dark Brown
- Portal 6 (Shadow): Black, Dark Gray, Dark Purple

STYLE: High contrast, clearly readable as portals. Swirling vortex patterns.
Dark, ominous aesthetic. Each visually distinct so player can identify what
type of monster will spawn. Pixel art, 32x32-48x48 size.
