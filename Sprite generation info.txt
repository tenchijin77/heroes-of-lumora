================================================================================
HEROES OF LUMORA - 2D Game Sprites
================================================================================

UNIVERSAL SPECIFICATIONS
========================
Art Style: Pixel art, 2D, modern flat top-down view (not isometric)
Base Size: 16x16 pixels per sprite (can be scaled up by AI)
Background: Solid color background (not transparent). Colored background will be removed
in post-processing.
Spacing: Each sprite centered with clear spacing around it for easy selection
Detail Level: Clean, readable at small scale, no tiny labels or text
Perspective: Flat overhead view looking straight down
No watermarks, or images other than the sprites

STYLE CONSISTENCY:
- All sprites should have consistent shading/lighting direction
- Use similar color depth and saturation across all sprites
- Maintain consistent line weight (pixel thickness)
- Keep anti-aliasing minimal for pixel art feel

TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS:
- Generate at 2x or 3x the final size (so 64x64 or 96x96) then scale down
- This maintains crispness and detail at small sizes
- Ensure transparency is clear (no white backgrounds)
- High contrast so sprites are readable at 32x32

COLOR RECOMMENDATIONS:
- Use vibrant but natural colors
- Avoid pure white (use off-white)
- Avoid pure black for outlines (use dark gray)
- Keep saturation high enough to be readable small

ORGANIZATION:
- Create each prompt separately for best results
- Request horizontal sprite strips (easier to work with)
- Ask AI to space sprites evenly
- Get high-res versions you can scale down
