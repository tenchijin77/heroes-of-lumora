Heroes of Lumora sprite sheet

2d game, pixel art, 32x32, modern style, flat top down view, not 3/4 view, on a transparent background, each section should be on a single sheet: NPCs, Monsters, Projectiles, Random, World. Each sprite should be centered, with spacing around it so it is easy to select the sprite, no labelling, No projectiles shown on the sprites; they'll be selected from the projectile section. Do not put names or labels on the sprites.
