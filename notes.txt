================================================================================
🗺️ LUMORA OUTSKIRTS - LEVEL DESIGN GUIDE
================================================================================

A detailed guide for terrain design, visual distinctions, natural transitions,
and NPC placement. This is your reference for drawing the map and planning 3D
models.

Levels 1-11 progression across three thematic sub-areas with natural transitions.


================================================================================
🏗️ OVERALL STRUCTURE & TERRAIN PHILOSOPHY
================================================================================

PRINCIPLE: "It should look like a real desert region that naturally progresses"

The zone flows from NORTH (safe city) → SOUTH (dangerous wilderness):
  • North: Flat, controlled, civilized edge
  • Center: Open sand, moderate challenge
  • South: Broken terrain, ruins, dangerous pockets
  • West: High dunes, environmental hazard
  • East: Undead concentration, cursed land

NATURAL TRANSITIONS:
  • No hard walls (except drop-off, which is geological)
  • Terrain gradually changes (dunes get bigger, vegetation dies off)
  • Mobs spawn in contextual clusters (not random)
  • Environmental hazards (heat, cliffs, ruins) create natural boundaries


================================================================================
🌅 SAND DUNES AREA (SAFE NORTH) - Levels 1-5
================================================================================

LOCATION: Immediately south of Northern Gate, spans roughly 40% of zone width

TERRAIN CHARACTERISTICS:
  • Gentle rolling dunes (10-15 ft height variation)
  • Sparse vegetation (dead shrubs, cacti clusters)
  • Sandy soil, easy to traverse
  • Occasional small rocks/stone markers
  • Clear sightlines (minimal cover)
  • Flat sky, bright and open

VISUAL PALETTE:
  • Sand color: Golden (#D4A574), warm beige (#C9B896)
  • Vegetation: Muted browns, grays
  • Sky: Clear blue-white (harsh sun overhead)
  • Lighting: Bright, no shadows from terrain

DISTINCTIVE FEATURES:
  • Small rocky outcrop (center-north) - natural gathering point
  • Stone markers/cairns - hints at old caravan routes
  • Dead shrub clusters - scattered groupings
  • Occasional small cactus garden - miniature oasis-like areas
  • Wide open sight lines - players feel safe

TERRAIN TRANSITIONS:
  • North edge: Guard towers visible (connects to city)
  • South edge: Dunes get slightly larger, more rugged
  • Gradual shift from "controlled" to "wild"
  • Dead vegetation becomes more common south of this area

ELEVATION MAP:
  • Flat to gently rolling
  • Max height: 15 ft above base
  • Natural valleys between dunes
  • No cliffs or major obstacles

NPC LOCATIONS:
  • Guard Post (north) - 2 guards, notice board
  • Rocky Outcrop (center) - social gathering spot
  • No permanent camps (those are further south)

MOB ENCOUNTER ZONES:
  • Mobs spawn scattered across open dunes
  • No dense clusters (teaching new players)
  • Easy to see and separate single mobs
  • Roaming mobs present (teaches kiting, awareness)

AUDIO/ATMOSPHERE:
  • Wind sounds (gentle, not harsh)
  • Occasional distant bird calls
  • Clear, bright feeling
  • No oppressive sounds


================================================================================
📍 DROP-OFF CLIFF - Levels 2-4
================================================================================

LOCATION: South edge of Sand Dunes, spans ~30% of zone width (center)

GEOGRAPHY:
  • 40-foot vertical cliff face
  • Geological stratification visible (layers of rock/sand)
  • Natural formation, looks ancient

SAFE PASSAGE OPTIONS:
  • Winding path: Switchbacks down cliff (safe, 2 min walk)
  • Ladder system: Built by merchants (safe, 1.5 min climb)
  • Midway landing: Small rest platform (flat area to catch breath)

VISUAL CHARACTERISTICS:
  • Dramatic stone face (gray/brown stratification)
  • Weathered, eroded edges
  • Sand drifts at base (accumulated over time)
  • Clear demarcation between "safe north" and "dangerous south"

FEATURES:
  • Warning sign at top (text tells players: "DANGER - Monsters Below")
  • Midway landing area (small flat platform, ~10x10 ft)
  • Rope/handholds visible (merchant-built infrastructure)
  • Path is clearly marked (worn by travelers)

LORE INDICATION:
  • Ancient geological shift created this barrier
  • Now a natural checkpoint for travelers
  • Merchants/scouts maintain the pathways

ENCOUNTERS:
  • Sparse (Level 2-4 mobs only)
  • Ambush opportunity: Bandits sometimes wait at base
  • Checkpoint feel - players pause here psychologically

NPC PLACEMENT:
  • Ambiguous NPC (scout or lost traveler) at midway landing
  • Optional flavor dialogue about the zone ahead
  • No combat here (protected area)


================================================================================
🌱 UNDEAD ROAMING AREA (MAIN ZONE) - Levels 5-11
================================================================================

This is your PRIMARY GAMEPLAY ZONE. It has multiple sub-regions with distinct
characteristics.

---

GENERAL CHARACTERISTICS:
  • Flat to gently rolling scrubland
  • Abandoned/ruined structures scattered throughout
  • Bones, skeletal trees, eerie atmosphere
  • Larger vegetation-free areas than north
  • More cover (ruins provide line-of-sight breaks)
  • Dead trees (skeletal, bleached white)

VISUAL PALETTE:
  • Sand color: Deeper gold (#C9A876), dusty beige
  • Dead vegetation: Bone white, dark brown
  • Stone ruins: Gray, weathered
  • Sky: Slightly warmer (orange-tinged vs pure blue)
  • Lighting: Still bright, but shadows more pronounced

ATMOSPHERIC FEELING:
  • Eerie, haunted, but not oppressive
  • "Something bad happened here"
  • Transition from safe to dangerous
  • Players should feel unease, but still capable

---

SUB-REGION 1: UNDEAD ROAMING CORE
  Location: Central area of Undead Roaming (levels 5-7 primary, 8-9 edges)

  Terrain:
    • Flat scrubland, scattered stones
    • Broken wagon wheels, torn cloth (abandoned caravans)
    • Crumbling stone foundations (old settlement ruins)
    • Dead trees (skeletal, no leaves)
    • Bone piles (scattered, visual indicator of undead)

  Features:
    • 3-4 ruined building foundations (walls only, no roofs)
    • Scattered wagon parts (wheels, axles, frames)
    • Rope/chains (what were they holding?)
    • Stone circle (ritual site? gathering place?)
    • Natural rock formation (provides some cover)

  Encounter Style:
    • Mobs spawn scattered and roaming
    • Mix of skeletons, goblins, bandits
    • Packs of 2-3 mobs common (teaches grouping)
    • Wandering predators appear randomly

  NPC Locations:
    • None (this is pure hunting zone)

---

SUB-REGION 2: GRAVEYARD (High Danger Pocket)
  Location: Southeast quadrant within Undead Roaming (levels 6-11)

  Geography:
    • Walled area (wrought iron fence, partially broken)
    • 20-30 weathered tombstones
    • 2 stone mausoleums (small building structures)
    • Fog effect (visual, atmospheric)
    • Stone benches (where corpses sit? eerie)
    • Ground is darker (stained? cursed? pulsing visual effect)

  Visual Distinction:
    • Clearly demarcated from surrounding area (fence/walls)
    • Cooler color tone (blues/grays vs warm yellows)
    • Fog particles (visual effect, no mechanical impact)
    • More stone structures (mausoleums, benches, markers)
    • Sparser vegetation (nothing grows here)

  Features:
    • Broken fence (multiple gaps for entry)
    • Readable tombstones (flavor text, lore)
    • Mausoleum doors (locked or ajar?)
    • Stone altar in center (ritual focus?)
    • Path of disturbed earth (grave robbing? undead rising?)

  Encounter Style:
    • DENSER mob spawns than core (it's a pocket)
    • Mobs stay mostly within graveyard (bound to location)
    • Less roaming (territorial)
    • Includes unique mob types (tomb ghouls, wights)

  NPC Locations:
    • None (sacred/cursed ground)

---

SUB-REGION 3: UNDEAD ROAMING DANGEROUS (Pockets)
  Location: Edge zones within Undead Roaming (levels 7-11, dangerous)

  Terrain:
    • Rougher than core (more rocks, broken terrain)
    • Deeper bone piles (more skeletal remains)
    • More ruins (settlement was larger than visible)
    • Rock formations (natural dungeons, caves)
    • Uneven ground (harder to traverse)

  Features:
    • Large rock outcrops (provide hiding spots for ambush)
    • Partially collapsed ruins (floor has holes)
    • Bone piles (deep, suggesting mass graves)
    • Dark shadows (crevices, caves)
    • Eerie atmosphere (players should feel danger)

  Encounter Style:
    • Mix of levels (7-9 common, 10-11 rare)
    • Ambush predators spawn here (Desert Predator)
    • Tougher elite variants of normal mobs
    • Higher mob density
    • Roaming mobs more aggressive

  NPC Locations:
    • None (avoid this area)


================================================================================
🏜️ HIGH DUNES AREA (Environmental Danger) - Levels 8-12
================================================================================

LOCATION: Southwest section, isolated and harsh

TERRAIN CHARACTERISTICS:
  • Massive sand dunes (30-50 ft height)
  • Extreme barrenness (no vegetation)
  • Some climbable dune ridges
  • Hard-packed sand (difficult traversal)
  • Rock formations breaking through sand
  • No shade anywhere (oppressive)

VISUAL PALETTE:
  • Sand color: Deep golden (#B8860B), rusty (#A0522D)
  • Sky: Harsh orange (heat shimmer effect)
  • Limited color range (monochromatic, oppressive)
  • Extreme contrast (bright sun, deep shadows in dunes)

ATMOSPHERIC FEELING:
  • Oppressive, dangerous, inhospitable
  • "The desert will kill you here"
  • Visual shimmer (heat haze effect)
  • Mirage pools (water that isn't there)
  • Sense of isolation

DISTINCTIVE FEATURES:
  • Towering dune ridges (30-50 ft high)
  • Scattered boulders (break line of sight)
  • Rocky outcrops (geological formations)
  • Mirage shimmer effect (visual)
  • No water anywhere (completely dry)
  • Occasional bones (large creatures)

ELEVATION MAP:
  • High variation (10-50 ft)
  • Multiple peaks
  • Valleys between dunes
  • Natural cover from large dunes
  • Difficult but not impossible traversal

ENVIRONMENTAL MECHANICS:
  • Heat Fatigue effect (stamina drain faster)
  • Reduced visibility (mirage effect)
  • Reduced movement speed (sand is hard to walk in)
  • No rest areas (hostile environment)

ENCOUNTER STYLE:
  • Fewer, tougher mobs (elite spawns)
  • Scattered, not grouped
  • Roaming predators common
  • Rare high-level spawns in corners
  • Environmental hazard IS the challenge

NPC LOCATIONS:
  • None (too dangerous)

💧 OASIS (Sacred Haven) - All Levels
================================================================================

LOCATION: Southwest corner, accessible from High Dunes or camps

TERRAIN CHARACTERISTICS:
  • Dramatic contrast to surroundings (lush vs barren)
  • Fresh water spring (centerpiece)
  • 15-20 palm trees (clustered naturally)
  • Clear water pool (visibly clean)
  • Smooth sand around water (walked bare by use)
  • Green vegetation (actual life)

VISUAL PALETTE:
  • Water: Clear blue (#1E90FF)
  • Vegetation: Vibrant green (#228B22), bright against desert
  • Sand: Lighter around water (well-traveled)
  • Sky: Still harsh above, but reflected in water
  • Lighting: Dappled through palm leaves

ATMOSPHERIC FEELING:
  • SANCTUARY
  • Peaceful relief from harsh desert
  • Safe, welcoming, healing
  • Music changes (gentle, calming)
  • Sound design: flowing water, rustling leaves

DISTINCTIVE FEATURES:
  • Central spring (actual water source)
  • Well (ancient, weathered, functional)
  • Smooth sand beach around water
  • Palm tree grove (provides shade)
  • Stone seating areas (natural gathering spots)
  • Clear, clean appearance (maintained by NPCs?)

MECHANICS:
  • No mobs (sacred ground, all factions respect this)
  • Water restores stamina (mechanical benefit)
  • Safe resting zone (no combat)
  • NPC interaction zone

NPC LOCATIONS:
  • Water Keeper (female merchant) - sells potions, rare herbs
  • Hermit Sage (old man) - offers wisdom
  • Occasional travelers (flavor NPCs)

LAYOUT:
  • Spring at center
  • Palms arranged naturally (some clustered, some scattered)
  • Smooth sand creates natural gathering area
  • Small stone well structure (functional)
  • Seating areas around water's edge

🏕️ DUSTWALKER CAMPS (Neutral Hubs) - 3 Locations
================================================================================

LOCATION 1: Northwest (between Sand Dunes and High Dunes)
LOCATION 2: Central-East (between Undead Area and Graveyard)
LOCATION 3: South (below High Dunes, near Oasis approach)

CAMP CHARACTERISTICS (per location):
  • Semi-permanent nomadic settlement
  • 5-8 nomadic tents (fabric, colorful, realistic)
  • Central campfire (gathering point)
  • Scattered sleeping mats
  • Merchant stall (small but functional)
  • Grazing animals (camels, horses - decorative)

VISUAL PALETTE:
  • Tent fabric: Colorful (reds, oranges, blues, earth tones)
  • Rope/posts: Natural wood/fiber
  • Campfire: Orange glow at night
  • Sand: Same as surrounding but clearly disturbed/packed
  • Animals: Earth tones (camels beige, horses brown)

ATMOSPHERIC FEELING:
  • Active, alive (people living here)
  • Friendly, hospitable
  • Cultural gathering point
  • Well-established but mobile (could pack up)

DISTINCTIVE FEATURES:
  • Central campfire (focal point)
  • Colorful tents (stands out from gray ruins)
  • Animal pens (ropes, posts)
  • Merchant stall (goods displayed)
  • Worn paths around camp (people living here)
  • Smell of cook fire (sensory)

TENT ARRANGEMENT:
  • Ring formation (around fire, defensive/communal)
  • Small entrance area
  • Merchant stall positioned for visibility
  • Animal grazing area offset slightly

NPC LOCATIONS per Camp:
  • Camp Leader (tent/central area) - quests, faction rep
  • Caravan Master (merchant stall) - buying/selling supplies
  • Dustwalker Scout (near edge) - wants info on monsters
  • 2-3 flavor NPCs (sitting by fire, tending animals)

ENCOUNTER ZONES:
  • Mobs: NONE inside camps
  • Edges: Level 2-3 mobs nearby (outer boundary)
  • Safe radius: ~50 ft around camp center

🏪 NEUTRAL VENDOR CAMP - Single Location
================================================================================

LOCATION: Northwest, between Sand Dunes and High Dunes

CAMP CHARACTERISTICS:
  • Established merchant settlement (more permanent than nomadic camps)
  • Large merchant tent (weathered canvas, reinforced)
  • Supply shack (wood and rope structure)
  • Trading post (wooden counter, shelving)
  • Neutral territory banners/markers
  • Comfortable seating area
  • No animals (merchant operation, not nomadic)

VISUAL PALETTE:
  • Canvas: Weathered white/tan (established, maintained)
  • Wood: Darker (treated lumber, more permanent)
  • Neutral banners: Gray/silver (faction-neutral)
  • Ground: Hard-packed, swept clean
  • Fencing: Low fence marking merchant territory
  • Lighting: Lanterns at night (more permanent infrastructure)

ATMOSPHERIC FEELING:
  • Commerce hub (busy but organized)
  • Professional, established
  • Safe, neutral ground
  • Gathering place for travelers/merchants

DISTINCTIVE FEATURES:
  • Large merchant tent (bigger than nomadic camps)
  • Trading counter (actual furniture, not just canvas)
  • Supply shack (separate building structure)
  • Neutral zone banners (clearly marked)
  • Organized layout (rows of goods)
  • Seating area (benches, comfortable)
  • Better maintained (swept, organized)

LAYOUT:
  • Main merchant tent (center)
  • Supply shack (adjacent)
  • Trading counter area (covered, ventilated)
  • Seating area (around cleared space)
  • Merchant stalls (displayed goods)
  • Perimeter banners (mark neutral ground)

NPC LOCATIONS:
  • Neutral Vendor (trading counter) - buys/sells everything
  • Supply Master (supply shack) - potions, reagents, supplies
  • Information Broker (tent area) - lore, rumors, hints
  • Adventurer NPC (seating area) - daily quest giver

ENCOUNTER ZONES:
  • Mobs: NONE (enforced by all factions)
  • Edges: Roaming mobs nearby but typically avoid
  • Safe radius: ~75 ft (larger than nomadic camps)

🔮 HIDDEN AREAS & SECRETS

  Location: Under/beside main oasis
  Access: Hidden passage (requires exploration to find)
  Characteristics:
    • Luminescent plants (bioluminescence)

    • Magical spring source (explains surface oasis)

SHIPWRECK IN SAND (Far South)
  Location: Half-buried in dunes (far south, isolated)
  Access: Visible if you know where to look (landmark visible)
  Characteristics:
    • Ancient ship half-submerged in sand
    • Broken mast, tattered sails
    • Treasure inside (accessible)
    • High-level mobs guard it
    • Implies ocean once covered area (climate change lore)
  Atmosphere: Eerie, mysterious, archaeological
  Purpose: Lore implication, treasure hunt quest

NOMADIC CARAVAN ROUTE (NPC Path)
  Location: Traveled path between camps
  Characteristics:
    • Worn ground (visible path)
    • Merchant caravans travel this route on schedule
    • Opportunity to ambush or protect
    • Dynamic NPC movement
  Atmosphere: Active, alive with merchant activity
  Purpose: Dynamic events, player choice (good/evil)

MOONLIGHT OASIS (Night-only Area)
  Location: Separate from main oasis (appears at night only)
  Access: Visible only during night cycle
  Characteristics:
    • Bioluminescent plants (ethereal beauty)

  Purpose: Night-time exploration, alternative experience


================================================================================
🚶 NATURAL FLOW & TRANSITIONS
================================================================================

PLAYER JOURNEY (Natural Progression):

1. Exit city (Northern Gate) → Enter Sand Dunes (safe)
2. Explore dunes, gather resources, learn combat
3. Approach Drop-Off Cliff, pause (psychological barrier)
4. Descend via path or ladder
5. Enter Undead Roaming Area (tone shift - eerie)
6. Explore ruins, scattered mobs, growing challenge
7. Discover Graveyard (higher danger pocket)
8. Travel west to Oasis (safe haven, rest)
9. Travel south to High Dunes (environmental hazard)
10. Return to central camps for trading
11. Later: Venture to Djahani Encampment (mysterious)

KEY PRINCIPLE: Players never feel "walled off" - they can explore freely but
naturally encounter danger as they move south/deeper. Terrain changes gradually,
not abruptly (except Drop-Off, which is geological feature).

VISUAL SIGNPOSTING:
  • Sand Dunes: Golden, open, bright (SAFE)
  • Drop-Off: Dramatic change in elevation (CHECKPOINT)
  • Undead Roaming: Ruins, bones, darker (WARNING)
  • Graveyard: Walled, fog, cooler tones (DANGEROUS)
  • Oasis: Green, water, bright (SANCTUARY)
  • High Dunes: Massive dunes, heat shimmer (HOSTILE)
  • Djahani: Black sand, war banners, torches (FOREIGN/THREAT)

🎨 TERRAIN SUMMARY FOR 3D MODELING
================================================================================

ZONE USES 5 DISTINCT TERRAIN TYPES:

1. SANDY DUNES (Sand Dunes Area, High Dunes)
   - Undulating terrain mesh
   - Sand texture (golden to rust colors)
   - Sparse cactus/shrub models (scattered)
   - Rock formations (low, rounded)

2. SCRUBLAND (Undead Roaming Core)
   - Flat to gentle rolling mesh
   - Sand/dirt texture (beige/tan)
   - Dead tree models (skeletal)
   - Ruined building meshes
   - Bone pile models

3. GRAVEYARD (Fenced Area)
   - Flat mesh (maintained ground)
   - Stone tile texture (graves)
   - Tombstone models (varied)
   - Mausoleum building meshes
   - Iron fence mesh (partial)
   - Fog particle effect

4. CLIFF FACE (Drop-Off)
   - Vertical mesh (geological)
   - Stone texture (stratified)
   - Path meshes (safe traversal)
   - Rope/ladder models

5. OASIS (Lush Area)
   - Flat mesh (cleared area)
   - Water mesh (reflective)
   - Palm tree models (clustered)
   - Grass/green vegetation
   - Well/stone structures

CAMP MODELS:
  - Tent structures (fabric, flapping)
  - Campfire (particles, glow)
  - Merchant stalls (wood/canvas)
  - Animals (camels, horses - decorative)
  - Banners (flapping)
