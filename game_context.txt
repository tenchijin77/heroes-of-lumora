Environmental Variables:

-I am using Godot 4.4.1. Updated to Godot 4.6 on 2/9/2026.

Files:
-snakecase is used for all file names, with all words in lowercase
-pascalcase is used for folders, (i.e. first letter capital like /Data)
-all variables should have their type explicitly called to avoid error messages
-when creating elements in scene files, i will use snakecase for all names, i.e. (sound file named enemy_attack)
-all tables are stored in json files in res://Data/
-all scripts are stored in res://Scripts/
-all scenes are stored in res://Scenes
-all assets like sounds and images are in res://Assets/
-user data is saved in user://saves/ (not yet implemented)
-my player information is in player.gd
-all scripts should start with a comment designating the script name, i.e. (# snake.gd)
-all json files should start with a comment designating the file name and purpose, i.e. ("_comment": "monsters.json - this table is used for monster's stats ")
-all functions in scripts should start with a comment, documenting what the function does, i.e. #Apply stats to character file
-all script files should use tab indentation, not space indentation

Collisions:

-all monsters are in the monsters node group for collisions (layer 2)
-the player is in the player node group for collisions (layer 1)
-player projectiles are on layer 3 for collisions
-enemy projectiles are on layer 4 for collisions
-environment is on layer 5 for collisions

Naming:
-scenes or levels are referred to as zones
-monsters are referred to as mobs (moving objects)

Behaviour:
-spawns are instantiated and handled by node_pool.gd
-mobs are randomly generated via enemy_spawner.tscn with enemy_spawner.gd
-projectiles are all based off of projectile.tscn and projectile.gd; each unique attack will have its own file i.e. arrow.tscn and .gd for the player's attack, and bone.tscn and gd, and fireball.tscn and gd, and axe.tscn and gd for the enemies' attacks.

Monsters:
-monsters logic is handled by monster.tscn (the script that all unique mobs will inherit from) and monsters.gd
-monsters will have their own scene based off of monster.tscn. i.e. skeleton.tscn; when monsters have unique stats or logic, they will have their own script like skeleton.gd
-skeleton.tscn and gd - fires projectile, bone.tscn
-wizard.tscn and gd - fires projectile, fireball.tscn
-balrog.tscn and gd - fires projectile with malfire.tscn
-beholder.tscn and gd - fires projectile with disintegration_ray.tscn
-ghost.tscn and gd - fires projectile with ghost_fire.tscn
-goblin.tscn and gd - fires projectile with dagger.tscn
-hezrou.tscn and gd - fires projectile with bile_spew.tscn
-lich.tscn and gd - fires projectile with death_bolt.tscn
-ogre.tscn and gd - fires projectile with boulder.tscn
-troll.tscn and gd - fires projectile with poison_bolt.tscn
-wraith.tscn and gd - fires projectile with life_drain.tscn


Controls:


Move: Arrow keys or WASD

Melee Attack: Left mouse click - calls arrow.tscn and gd


================================================================================
HEROES OF LUMORA - GAME DESCRIPTION
================================================================================

SHORT DESCRIPTION (One-Liner)
-----------------------------
A solo-developed medieval fantasy tower defense bullet-hell hybrid where you
defend the town of Lumora from an endless siege of monsters while evacuating
villagers to safety.


MEDIUM DESCRIPTION (App Store/Game Jam Format)
-----------------------------------------------
Heroes of Lumora is a fast-paced medieval fantasy action game that blends
tower defense and bullet-hell mechanics. Stand as the last line of defense
for the town of Lumora as waves of monsters breach the gates. Evacuate 50
villagers to safety while fending off creatures of the Umbral Talon.

Team up with allies including the bard Annadaeus—who buffs your abilities
through inspiring songs—and the powerful archmage Tenchijin, who arrives
to turn the tide when darkness reaches its peak. Survive the waves, manage
your resources, and prepare yourself for the ultimate confrontation:
Mh'orzath, the Eclipsed One—an ancient cosmic horror that emerges when
the barriers between worlds grow thin.

Control with keyboard and mouse or gamepad. Defend. Survive. Triumph.


LONG DESCRIPTION (Website/Press Kit Format)
---------------------------------------------
HEROES OF LUMORA
A Medieval Fantasy Tower Defense Action Game

================================================================================
THE STORY
================================================================================

At dawn, the gates of Lumora shatter. A tide of shadow and teeth pours in—
creatures of the Umbral Talon, servants of an elder darkness that hungers
to consume the town and all within it. The town's defenders rally, but they
cannot hold alone. You arrive as the light breaks over the ridge, and Timot
of the market—a keeper of what few treasures remain—looks at you with
desperate hope.

"We need you to buy us time."

Fifty souls must reach the extraction points. Every second you hold the line
is another life that escapes. Every wave you survive is another breath drawn
by someone you save. But as the night deepens and the waves intensify,
something darker stirs—a presence so ancient and terrible that even the
monsters fear it.

When hope seems lost, Anna's voice rings out. The bard Annadaeus joins the
fight, her songs bolstering your strength and healing your wounds. And when
the battle reaches its crescendo, the Archmage Tenchijin himself materializes
to turn the tide.

But even with allies at your side, one final horror awaits: Mh'orzath,
the Eclipsed One—a being from the void between worlds, drawn by the
weakening seal beneath Lumora's walls.

================================================================================
GAMEPLAY
================================================================================

CONTROL & COMBAT
- Move with WASD keys, arrow keys, or left analog stick
- Aim with mouse or right analog stick
- Fire with left mouse button or right trigger
- Stay mobile. Stay alive.

DEFEND THE TOWN
- Evacuate 50 villagers to extraction points
- Protect them from monsters that hunt them at the gates
- Use Solari (in-game currency) to purchase upgrades and defensive structures
- Call upon guards and priestesses to aid in the fight

ALLY WITH HEROES
- Annadaeus: The Bard — Joins automatically. Sings buffs that boost your
  damage, speed, and healing. Manage her cooldowns.

- Tenchijin: The Archmage — Arrives when the battle turns darkest (30% Anna
  health, Anna's death, or wave 20+). Casts devastation spells: Abyss Touch,
  Event Horizon, and Nihil Storm.

FACE THE BOSS
When 50 villagers escape, all lesser monsters vanish. Mh'orzath rises.

This cosmic horror fights in three devastating phases:
- **Abyss Touch** — Fast void projectiles that home toward you
- **Event Horizon** — A gravity well that pulls you toward the boss and deals
  damage over time
- **Nihil Storm** — A screen-wide ultimate attack that devastates everything

Defeat Mh'orzath to save Lumora.

✓ NPC RESCUE MECHANICS

  - Protect extraction points from assault
  - Witness the consequences of your defense (or failure to defend)

  - Unique attack patterns requiring different strategies
  - Victory screen with detailed stats and developer message

================================================================================
WORLD & LORE
================================================================================

Lumora is one corner of a vast world called Aldenexia. This medieval town
sits above an ancient seal imprisoning a cosmic horror—Mh'orzath, the
Eclipsed One. When the seal weakens, shadows seep through, and the darkness
begins its hunger.

The characters you meet—Anna, Tenchijin, and others—are threads in a much
larger tapestry. Their stories, struggles, and powers hint at deeper mysteries
in Aldenexia's history.

Heroes of Lumora is both a complete game AND a window into that larger world.
It serves as a brief introduction to Aldenexia: Lightfall, an upcoming full
game that will explore this realm in depth.

This is Ross Wilkinson's first game—a solo project born from months of

Heroes of Lumora is the beginning of a larger vision. The next chapter of

If Lumora earned your sword, I hope Aldenexia will earn your return.

KEY THEMES

LIGHT vs. DARKNESS
The eternal struggle between hope (golden light) and despair (void shadow).

SACRIFICE & SALVATION
Every villager you save has a name, a story. Protecting them drives the

RISING DARKNESS
The threat escalates from minor skirmishes to waves of deadly monsters,
culminating in the awakening of an ancient evil.

HEROIC ALLIANCE
You don't stand alone. Allies arrive when needed most. Together, heroes can

================================================================================
VISUAL STYLE
================================================================================

Medieval fantasy pixel/sprite-based art with a cosmic horror twist. The town
of Lumora is vibrant and lived-in, filled with torches and defense structures.
The monsters grow darker and more otherworldly as waves progress.

The climax features Mh'orzath—a being of pure void and shadow, larger than
comprehension, wrapped in purple cosmic energies and writhing tentacles.

The color palette shifts from warm medieval golds and oranges to cold purples
and blacks as the eclipse descends, signaling the player's progress toward
the final confrontation.
